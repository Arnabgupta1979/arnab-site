About

Seeds, people
and the systems between them.

I am a researcher and Coordinating Data Steward for the Social Sciences Group of Wageningen University & Research, based at Wageningen Social and Economic Research (WSER). My work connects seed systems, practical digital tools and responsible use of data.

My story

From seed science to
digital decision tools.

I trained in Seed Science and Technology at the Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI), New Delhi. My earlier commercial work at Incotec and Nusun Genetic Research brought me into seed enhancement, laboratory development and quality assurance.

At Bioversity International in New Delhi, from 2014 to 2018, I worked on agroecology and seed systems: participatory varietal selection, crop diversification, community seed banks and farmer-led adaptation. Citizen science and farmers’ rights were part of that work.

The Myanmar chapter.

In 2018, I moved to Pathein, Myanmar, as Senior Seed Sector Advisor with Deutsche Welthungerhilfe. I managed the Rice Seed Sector Development project in the Ayeyarwady Delta, working on modern seed laboratories, quality systems, digital logistics and seed demand–supply intelligence. Training government, NGO and seed-sector colleagues, and developing a strategic roadmap for the rice seed sector, were central to that role.

I joined WUR in 2019 at the Wageningen Centre for Development Innovation (WCDI) and moved to WSER in 2023, where I now work in the Data Science & Innovation team. My work includes locally hosted AI advisory prototypes, seed demand forecasting, seed tracking and the governance of research data. As Coordinating Data Steward for the Social Sciences Group, I advise research teams on data management, ethics and the responsible use of AI. Until 2025, I co-led ISSD Africa’s action learning on resilient seed systems. Myanmar remains part of my work: I am WUR’s technical lead for STREAM’s Mandalay seed system resilience assessment, working with Welthungerhilfe and local partners.

Across Africa and Asia, I work with ministries, NGOs, CGIAR partners and practitioners. This has included support for community seed banks in Somaliland, South Sudan, Sudan and Myanmar, and helping enable a transfer of CIAT genebank material to Somaliland. More recent work covers seed-market inspection in Bangladesh and circular cacao value chains linking Ecuador and the European Union. The aim is practical: information and seed systems that people can use in their own contexts.

Alongside research, I write books and essays about evidence, history, food, culture and life with AI.
