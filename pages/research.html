Research

Evidence for
practical decisions.

My research connects seed systems, agricultural diversity, digital advisory tools and data governance. This page presents selected publications and knowledge products, rather than a complete bibliography.

Research themes

Questions that guide the work.

Seed systems & resilience

Seed system resilience assessments, community seed banks, farmer-managed systems, crop diversification and seed quality assurance, including work in fragile contexts.

Digital & AI tools

Seed demand forecasting, seed tracking, dashboards and locally hosted RAG advisory prototypes that retrieve information from a defined set of sources.

Data stewardship

Research data management, FAIR practices, DPIAs and data-sharing agreements, informed by GDPR, the EU AI Act and the Data Governance Act.

Tools & methods

Practical research infrastructure.

I work with local AI tools, research data workflows and field survey methods. Tool choice follows the question and the context.

  • Local AI: Ollama, Mistral, FAISS / ChromaDB, Streamlit and Docker
  • Data collection and analysis: KoBo / ODK, KNIME and Python at a basic level
  • Data stewardship: FAIR, DataCite, Dublin Core and evaluation of Yoda / iRODS
  • Visualisation and citizen science: Power BI, Looker and ClimMob
