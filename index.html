Career arc

From seed science
to systems that work.

My path runs through IARI, commercial seed enhancement and quality assurance, and research with Bioversity International in India.

Myanmar was an important chapter. In 2018–2019, I was based in Pathein with Welthungerhilfe, managing rice seed sector development in the Ayeyarwady Delta. Seed laboratories, quality systems, digital logistics and training connected the science with everyday sector decisions.

At WUR since 2019, I work on agrifood information systems, data stewardship, locally hosted AI advisory and resilient seed systems.
